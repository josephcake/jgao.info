# Level 1: THE LAST MILE

Seven rooms inside the WiFi modem, then a boss. Pix starts hollow and pitch black;
the first port he jacks in 1-1 gives his blue back. There is **no attack and there
are no enemies**: the only things out to get Pix are spikes, gaps, and the boss, and
the boss is out-thought, not fought.

Pix has run, jump, double jump, dash, wall-jump, planks, zip ports (green, tap to
pull yourself in) and the blue lens. No swing, no power, no hack yet.

| Room | Flow | Teaches / tests |

## Boss: SNAKE.EXE (3 crashes)

A computer worm that plays Snake on the arena grid, with Pix as the apple. It steers
around its own body and the walls, so only a crash hurts it. Pix never touches it
except to get bitten. The trick is to make it crash.

- **Fire the cable** (jack, at anything or nothing) and any head near Pix flinches:
  it turns on its next step without looking. Fire it when that turn leads into its
  own body, a wall, or the other worm. There's a short cooldown.
- **Phase 1:** one worm. Make it bite itself.
- **Phase 2:** it replicates into two worms that don't avoid each other. Make them collide.
- **Phase 3:** fileless. The body is only visible in true view (hold the lens).

Training Grounds is still on the title menu: five rooms of movement, power and swing
practice, with every mechanic unlocked.

## Hack and yank (added)

Stage 1 now teaches every cable action except swing and power, with practice first:

- **1-3 OPEN PORT** (hack practice, no spikes): purple data ports open gates for good.
- **1-4 PULL REQUEST** (yank practice, no spikes): orange ports are levers. Yank to carry an
  orange-striped slider (up, or across a pit) or to open a door. A yank toggles; yank again to
  send it back. Only striped platforms are yankable.
- **1-9 CHAIN OF COMMAND**: yank a bridge over spikes, hack a gate, hop spikes, zip up a wall,
  yank the last door. Needs all of it, with spikes back.

`Abilities.ts` unlocks hack and yank at level 1, swing and power at level 2.
